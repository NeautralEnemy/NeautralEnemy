# Feature Ideas (Round 2)

The following ten ideas range from simple polish to mid-scope mechanics that would broaden Colonial Command's strategic depth
and replayability without demanding major refactors.

1. **Seasonal Weather Modifiers** – Apply light bonuses/penalties to movement and battles based on the current season (e.g., winter slows marches, summer improves naval range).
2. **Province Ledger Screen** – Add a tabular summary accessible from the campaign UI that lists regions, income, stability, and supply status for quick empire management.
3. **Army Stance Toggles** – Let armies switch between Aggressive, Balanced, and Defensive postures that tweak upkeep, movement, and battle initiative.
4. **Naval Patrol Routes** – Allow players to assign fleets to sea lanes to guard trade routes, granting bonus income and reducing enemy raid chances.
5. **Espionage Actions** – Introduce a simple spy unit that can scout fogged regions or attempt sabotage events with risk/reward outcomes.
6. **Governor Traits** – Attach rotating trait bonuses to region governors (auto-assigned leaders) that buff stability, income, or recruitment in their province.
7. **Expanded Diplomacy Offers** – Add timed peace truces, alliance offers, and tribute demands to make negotiations more dynamic.
8. **Capital Upgrade Track** – Provide a 3-step capital city upgrade that boosts supply cap, recruitment slots, and defensive strength.
9. **Battle Outcome Journal** – Maintain a running journal of major battles with casualties and rewards accessible from the menu for storytelling.
10. **Scenario Challenge Mode** – Offer curated starting setups (e.g., “Defend the Caribbean”) selectable from the menu with unique win conditions.

Each feature dovetails with existing systems (supply, diplomacy, movement) and can be implemented incrementally while delivering
noticeable improvements to the gameplay loop.
