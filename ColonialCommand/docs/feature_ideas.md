# Feature Ideas (Simple to Medium)

Below are ten potential features that range from quick UI polish to medium-sized mechanics enhancements. Each idea is scoped to be feasible without major refactors while still adding noticeable value for Colonial Command.

1. **Campaign Objectives Tracker** – Add a sidebar widget that lists 2–3 dynamic objectives (e.g., hold 5 regions, maintain 80% stability) with checkmarks when completed, giving players short-term goals.
2. **Recruitment Queue Preview** – Display upcoming units in a mini queue on the region panel so players can see what will complete in the next few turns.
3. **Event Pop-ups with Choices** – Introduce lightweight narrative events that present a brief story snippet and two options affecting stability, income, or diplomacy.
4. **Resource Trade Routes UI** – Visualize existing trade agreements on the campaign map with subtle animated lines between regions to clarify economic relationships.
5. **Tactical Terrain Modifiers** – Add 2–3 tile types (forest, hill, fort) that provide simple attack/defense modifiers in tactical battles.
6. **Army Experience System** – Track battle participation to award small bonuses (+attack/+morale) after repeated victories, encouraging unit preservation.
7. **Expanded Research Notifications** – Provide a celebratory banner and sound cue when a technology completes, with a quick link to queue the next research.
8. **Advisor Tips Panel** – Create an optional panel that surfaces contextual advice (e.g., low treasury, idle research) using existing message log infrastructure.
9. **Custom Skirmish Setup** – Allow players to start a quick tactical battle by selecting factions and unit compositions from the main menu for sandbox testing.
10. **Fog-of-War Reveal Animations** – When discovering a new region, play a short radial wipe and update the map with a shimmer effect to celebrate exploration.

These ideas can be tackled individually and provide a mix of presentation, usability, and gameplay depth improvements.
